## Personal effects — guidelines & safekeeping

Maintaining a safe, comfortable community at Monarch Competency requires thoughtful limits on personal items.

**Upon arrival**, our team conducts a respectful review of your belongings to support community safety. Some items may be securely stored with staff for occasional use or safekeeping.

**We recommend** bringing essential, meaningful items only. Compact belongings that store easily help you and your roommate share the space comfortably.

**Valuables:** We recommend limiting valuables during your stay; Monarch cannot guarantee against loss or damage.

**Prohibited items** will be placed in a secured, locked location and returned at discharge to you or your designated support person, as appropriate.

**Not sure about an item?** Call admissions before you arrive — we can confirm whether it is permitted.

## Clothing & personal presentation

Personal hygiene and appropriate dress support your growth and a healthy shared living environment.

**Clothing is not permitted** if it depicts violence, gang affiliation, or drug use; shows nudity; is torn or styled to be intentionally revealing; or is otherwise overtly offensive.

**Pack for the season** — include layers or warmer clothing when Colorado weather requires it.

## Your responsibilities during your stay

- **Living space & linens:** Keep your room, linens, and clothing clean and orderly.
- **House chore:** Complete your assigned weekly house chore as part of community living.
- **Medications:** See below — all medications are managed by staff.

---

## Medications

Any medication you bring will be:

1. **Inventoried** by Monarch staff upon arrival  
2. **Stored securely** in a locked location  
3. **Administered** during our **three scheduled medication times** each day  

Prescriptions written or renewed during your stay are handled the same way. **Do not keep medication in your room** unless staff explicitly instruct otherwise for a documented clinical reason.

If you have questions about a specific medication or supplement, contact admissions before arrival.
