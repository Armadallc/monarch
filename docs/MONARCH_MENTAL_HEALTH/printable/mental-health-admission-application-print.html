Monarch Mental Health is a level two Mental Health Transitional Living Home (MHTL). We are dedicated to providing comprehensive and client-centered care for individuals transitioning from various settings, including state hospitals and other institutions, to community living. Monarch offers a supportive environment where clients can reintegrate into society with the necessary therapeutic support. We provide individual therapy services, group therapy sessions, comprehensive case management, medication management, and other recreational therapy services.

Monarch Mental Health accepts Medicaid/Medicare insurance only. Please note, private insurance is not accepted.
