Referral to Monarch Mental Health

Colorado · For referring professionals and authorized representatives

Submit this referral by:

Email the completed form and any attachments to referrals@monarchmentalhealth.org, or call 1 (800) 618-8719 (Admissions ext. 0). Secure online submission is also available on our website.

We accept incomplete referrals. Send what you have now; you can send the rest later. Attachments help us decide faster.

Not sure about a question? Call us at 1 (800) 618-8719 (Admissions ext. 0) or email referrals@monarchmentalhealth.org. We are glad to help you complete this form.

Instructions:  Type in the fields, or print and use black or blue ink. If an item does not apply, write “N/A” or leave it blank. Check every box that applies. Items labeled “mark one”, check only one option.
